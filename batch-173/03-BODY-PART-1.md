# BLOCK 2 — THE PROBLEM (the stack) · 0:20–1:15 · S01–S14

**Block intent:** build the graveyard by accumulation, then turn it from funny to sad in one shot (`S09`), then
make it *cost a marriage a little* (`S12`–`S14`). Cut fast — 2–3s — so the bottles pile up on the viewer the way
they piled up on her. Then stop dead at `S14`.

**Grade for the whole block:** GRADE-A. Flat, cool, desaturated, lifted blacks. Nothing warm until 1:45.
