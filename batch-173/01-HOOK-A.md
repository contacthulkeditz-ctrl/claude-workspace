# HOOK A (H1) — 0:00–0:20 · 7 shots

> "I took twenty-three supplements every morning for eight years and never once felt fixed. The thing that
> finally worked was forgetting the entire bag on a trip and not taking a single one for five days. By day
> three, I slept through the night for the first time in two years."

**Hook strategy:** open on the most literal, most recognisable image in the entire avatar's life — a palm full of
mismatched pills. No face for the first 3 seconds. Faces in frame 1 read as "ad"; hands read as "someone's
life." Her face arrives at 0:06 exactly when the VO turns emotional ("never once felt fixed"), so the first
thing you ever see of her is disappointment, not a greeting.

Prompts below assume `GLOBAL_PREFIX` + `GLOBAL_SUFFIX` + `NEG` from the bible.

### H1-07 · 0:17.5–0:20 · 2.5s · HOOK PAYOFF
> **VO:** "By day three, I slept through the night for the first time in two years."

**Why:** The promise, delivered as confusion rather than joy. She isn't happy yet — she's *disoriented*. Real
people don't wake up grateful, they wake up going "wait, what time is it?" That's the beat that reads as true.

**IMAGE** — *GPT Image*
```

on the nightstand reading 7:04. Broad warm morning sun falls in a hard band across the
quilt and the lower half of her face. Her expression is confusion, not happiness — brows
slightly drawn, mouth relaxed and open a few millimetres. 50mm, f/2.0, camera low at

```

**MOTION (I2V)**
```
Camera holds at mattress height with the faintest handheld float. Her eyes refocus on the
clock. She blinks twice, slowly. Her brow lifts very slightly as it registers. She does not

```
