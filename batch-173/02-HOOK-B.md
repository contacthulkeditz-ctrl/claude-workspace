# HOOK B (H2) — 0:00–0:20 · 6 shots · SWAP-TEST ALTERNATE

> "I forgot my supplement bag on a five-day trip. By day three I slept through the night for the first time in
> two years, and I came home wondering which of my twenty-three bottles had been making me worse."

**How this differs from Hook A:** H1 opens on the *stack* and travels to the accident. H2 opens on the *accident*
and travels back to the stack. So H2 must open on a body-language moment — the exact half-second of "oh no" — and
end on the row of bottles as an open question. Same character, same wardrobe rules, same grades. Only the order
of revelation changes.

Do not remix these two hooks. Run them as clean swaps against the identical body so the test reads clean.

---

### H2-01 · 0:00–0:04 · 4s · COLD OPEN
> **VO:** "I forgot my supplement bag"

**Why:** Open on the drop. A 4am airport, a hand going into a bag that doesn't contain the thing, and a face
going still. Everyone knows that feeling in their body before they know it in words — which is why it stops a
scroll faster than any object shot.

**IMAGE** — *GPT Image*
```
W3. L5 — airport concourse at 4am, almost deserted. Medium shot of the woman stopped dead
mid-stride, three-quarter front, her right hand pushed deep into an open tan crossbody bag
against her hip, feeling for something. Her carry-on has rolled slightly past her and its
handle is tilting. She is looking down and slightly to the left at nothing, mouth parted a
few millimetres, brow tightening — the half-second before a realisation becomes a sentence.
Cold overhead terminal fluorescents with a warm sodium spill from a distant lit newsstand
behind her. Blurred empty concourse recedes deep behind. 50mm, f/2.0, eye level, she sits
in the right third with the empty concourse open to her left. GRADE-B: cold blue-grey,
deep shadows, one warm accent.
```

**MOTION (I2V)**
```
Slow 10cm push in. Her hand searches the bag twice more, then stops moving entirely. Her
shoulders drop. Her eyes stay fixed on nothing. The carry-on handle finishes tipping and
settles. A single blurred traveller crosses the deep background. She never looks at the
camera. 4 seconds, real-time.
```
**→ hands off:** `LIGHT-WIPE` — a foreground blur passes camera-left as the traveller clears; cut inside
it to the dark kitchen.

### H2-03 · 0:07–0:11 · 4s · PAYOFF
> **VO:** "By day three I slept through the night"

**Why:** Same disorientation beat as H1-07 and for the same reason — confusion sells, gratitude doesn't. Here it
lands earlier in the hook, so hold it a half-second longer to let it register.

**IMAGE** — *GPT Image*
```
W2. L6 — sister's guest bedroom. The woman lying on her side in a patterned quilt, head on

on her cheek. She is looking across the bed at a small brass alarm clock on the nightstand
reading 7:04. A hard band of warm morning sun lies across the quilt and the lower half of
her face. Expression is confusion, not happiness — brows slightly drawn, mouth relaxed and
barely parted. 50mm, f/2.0, camera low at mattress height. GRADE-E: warm returning
daylight, open shadows.
```

**MOTION (I2V)**
```
Camera holds at mattress height with the faintest handheld float. Her eyes refocus on the
clock. She blinks twice, slowly, and her brow lifts very slightly. She pushes up onto one
elbow about ten centimetres and stops there. No smile. Dust motes drift in the sunlight.
4 seconds, real-time.
```
**→ hands off:** `MATCH-SHAPE` — cut on the clock face to a phone screen in darkness at the same
position. Hard tonal reversal, same geometry.

### H2-04 · 0:11–0:14 · 3s · CONTRAST FLASH
> **VO:** "for the first time in two years,"

**Why:** You can't feel "first time in two years" without seeing the two years. One flash-cut of 3:12am ceiling-
staring, blue phone light on her face, does more than any line of VO. Shoot it colder and flatter than everything
around it so it reads instantly as *before*.

**IMAGE** — *Nano Banana Pro*
```
W2. L3 — her own bedroom, deep night. Overhead shot from directly above the bed, looking
straight down. The woman lies flat on her back on oatmeal linen, both eyes wide open,
staring straight up past the camera, completely still. Her phone lies face-up on the duvet
beside her hand, its screen the only light source, throwing a hard cold blue-white glow up
across her chin, cheekbones and the underside of her brow, leaving her eye sockets in
shadow. The rest of the room is near-black. The phone screen reads 3:12. 35mm, f/2.8,
directly overhead. Very cold, very flat, crushed blacks.
```

**MOTION (I2V)**
```
Locked-off overhead, absolutely no camera move. She does not move at all except for one
slow blink at the very end. The phone screen dims by one brightness step halfway through.
Utter stillness — the shot should feel airless. 3 seconds.
```
**→ hands off:** `FOCUS-HANDOFF` — the phone glow blooms out to soft white; open H2-05 on a soft
overexposed doorway and rack in.

### H2-05 · 0:14–0:17 · 3s
> **VO:** "and I came home wondering"

**Why:** She's home, coat still on, suitcase still in hand, standing in the bathroom doorway looking at the
counter. Nobody unpacks first. That detail is what makes it read as a real memory instead of a staged scene.

**IMAGE** — *GPT Image*
```

shoulder line. Framed by the dark doorjamb, the bright bathroom beyond, and on the counter
the long row of supplement bottles catching the window light. She is completely motionless,
looking at the counter. 35mm, f/2.8, eye level, deep frame-within-a-frame composition.
GRADE-A returning: flat, cool, desaturated.

**MOTION (I2V)**

Very slow 15cm push forward through the doorway, past her shoulder, so the counter of

camera. 3 seconds.
```
**→ hands off:** `PAN-THROUGH` — the camera is already pushing forward; H2-06 opens continuing that same
push along the counter at the same rate. Should read as one unbroken move.

### H2-06 · 0:17–0:20 · 3s · OPEN LOOP
> **VO:** "which of my twenty-three bottles had been making me worse."

**Why:** End the hook on an unanswered question with her out-of-focus behind the evidence. The bottles are sharp,
she is soft — the objects are in charge of her life, and the frame says so. Do not resolve this shot; the
unresolved rack is the retention device.

**IMAGE** — *Nano Banana Pro*
```
W3. L1 — master bathroom. Low camera at counter height, looking straight down the long row
of twenty-plus supplement bottles receding into the frame, labels facing every direction,
one tipped on its side. The nearest three bottles are razor sharp. Deep behind them and
completely out of focus, the woman stands at the far end of the counter, an unreadable
soft shape with her arms folded. Flat cool window light camera-left. 50mm, f/1.8, focus
locked on the nearest bottle. GRADE-A: desaturated, greenish-grey shadows, low contrast.
```

**MOTION (I2V)**
```
Slow continuous 12cm dolly forward along the row of bottles. Halfway through, the focus
begins to rack off the bottles toward her blurred figure but stops before it resolves —
she never comes fully sharp. She shifts her weight once in the blur. 3 seconds.
```
**→ hands off into body:** `PAN-THROUGH` — S01 opens on the same counter, wide, camera still drifting.
The body picks up mid-move.
